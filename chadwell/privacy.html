Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the lesson inquiry form:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, if you choose to give one
  • who the lessons are for, what you're looking for, and any message you write — including anything you choose to share about adjustments that would help

How it's used

Your details are used only by Sam Chadwell to reply to your inquiry. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to Sam.

Fonts and scripts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, send a message through the lesson inquiry form and mention it in your message.

Images

Sam's own photos haven't been added yet. Until they are, the images on this site are illustrations made for the site and one stock photo of tennis equipment. No person shown or implied on the site is presented as Sam or as one of his players.
