Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the coaching inquiry form:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, if you choose to give one
  • what you're interested in, how you'd describe your current experience, and any message you write

How it's used

Your details are used only by David Mott to reply to your inquiry. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to David.

Fonts and scripts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, send a message through the coaching inquiry form and mention it in your message.

Photos

Photos of David on this site are his own. Other people who appear in them are not identified, and nothing on the site describes them as his students. A few equipment and court-detail photos are free stock images.
